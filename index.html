SBDC Executive Insights: Sourcing Opportunities

Central Indiana's Biomedical, Biotechnical, and Healthcare cluster demonstrates strong regional self-sufficiency (60.85%), but imports $5.36B of purchases annually. SBDC Business Advisors can target specific supply chain leakages—primarily in Drugs and Druggists' Sundries ($1.20B imported) and Medicinal and Botanical Manufacturing ($825M imported)—to drive import substitution, coordinate local partnerships, and attract strategic suppliers to Indiana.
